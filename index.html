Dust Shift

Third shift at the plant. Combustible dust is settling faster than the crew can sweep it, and you're the one on the hose. Drag it to a wall drop, plug in, pull the floor clean. Last ten minutes and the plant makes it to morning.

Controls

  • WASD walk — you turn as you go, and the wand holds that direction
  • E connect at a drop — and walk back to it to unplug
  • Hold click or Space to vacuum
  • Q and R fire the Suck Norris and FiberLight, once earned
  • M mutes — P pauses — Esc ends the shift

The job

  • Suction only runs while you're plugged into a drop.
  • Walk too far and the hose goes taut — it won't stretch.
  • Every pile left on the floor drives the dust level up.
  • Lift trucks and the manager's golf cart will crush your hose and flatten you. Stay out of the lanes.
  • At 100% the alarm sounds. Get out before it goes.

Upgrades

  • Every pound you recover fills the upgrade meter. Fill it and the rep shows up with three cards.
  • Clearing a whole spill drops a bonus straight into the meter.
  • Hose length and hose size, extra drops, horsepower, Dust Dozer, Pile Diver, FiberLight, and coffee. They stack.
